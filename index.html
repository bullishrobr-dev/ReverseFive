Activation mechanism

Yubari King requires a minimum 5-hour activation window after application. During this period, the treatment interacts with your skin's natural processes. Do not wash or rub the treated areas during this activation window.

After the 5-hour minimum, you may continue with your normal skincare routine. The formulation is designed to work progressively with consistent weekly use.

Key ingredient system

Peptides

Short chains of amino acids that signal your skin to strengthen its own support structure. They help improve the visible appearance of firmness.

Hyaluronic Acid

A molecule found naturally in skin that holds up to 1,000 times its weight in water. It provides surface hydration support and helps maintain skin moisture balance during the correction process.

Stem Cell Complex

Plant-derived compounds that support your skin's natural renewal activity and visible refinement over time.

Important guidelines

Follow these guidelines to ensure optimal conditions for the treatment protocol.

Do

  • Apply to clean, dry skin
  • Allow minimum 5-hour activation window
  • Apply once per week only
  • Use precision application to target areas
  • Follow consistent weekly schedule

Avoid

  • Washing face during activation window
  • Rubbing or touching treated areas
  • Exceeding once-weekly frequency
  • Applying to deep nasolabial folds
  • Internal use (external only)

External use only

Yubari King is designed exclusively for external application. Do not ingest or apply to mucous membranes or broken skin. If irritation occurs, discontinue use and consult a healthcare professional.
